Do's & Don'ts

Do

  • Keep amber as the only expressive color; let weight and warm neutrals carry hierarchy.
  • Temperature-tint every neutral warm — no pure grays, no #000, no #F2F2F2.
  • Bind visual values to tokens; rationalize new sizes onto the spacing/type scale.
  • Give pills a ≥44px hit area and a visible amber focus ring.
  • Use elevation tokens for all shadows; hold bio copy to ~62ch.

Don't

  • Add a second accent hue or any gradient — not on heroes, not on buttons.
  • Reintroduce amber-tinted glow shadows or colored hover halos.
  • Use glassmorphism; lifted surfaces are solid surfaceRaised.
  • Use textFaint for body or essential text — decorative icons only.
  • Invent a variant/state or hardcode a value that has a token.
